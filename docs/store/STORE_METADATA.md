> HAPA is the marketplace built for Embu, Kenya.
>
> • Hire verified local professionals — fundis, tutors, tailors, salonists and
>   more — with real reviews from your community.
> • Shop from local businesses: browse listings, send enquiries, place orders
>   and arrange pickup or delivery.
> • Book transport the easy way: set your exact pickup point on the map, choose
>   your destination, and agree the price directly with a verified driver.
> • Chat inside every request, track its status, and review providers after the
>   job is done.
>
