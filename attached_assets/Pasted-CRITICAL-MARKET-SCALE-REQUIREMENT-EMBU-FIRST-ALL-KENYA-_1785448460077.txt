CRITICAL MARKET-SCALE REQUIREMENT — EMBU FIRST, ALL KENYA NEXT

HAPA must launch first as a controlled pilot in Embu, but the architecture must be Kenya-wide from day one.

Do not hardcode Embu into the database, dispatch engine, fare engine, marketplace, URLs, driver eligibility or user profiles.

Implement a configurable geographic hierarchy:

- country
- county
- sub-county
- town/city
- service zone
- pickup and destination coordinates

Initial configuration:

- Country: Kenya
- First active county: Embu
- First active service zone: Embu pilot area

All other Kenyan counties may remain disabled until the Owner activates them.

Required nationwide scalability:

1. Service areas
- configurable counties, towns and geofenced service zones
- Owner can activate or pause each area
- providers and drivers select approved operating areas
- customers can search by county, town and current location
- cross-county trips may be enabled through configuration

2. Dispatch
- driver matching must operate inside the selected service zone
- search radius and expansion rules configurable by zone
- no driver from an inactive area may receive offers
- location logic must work anywhere within Kenya

3. Pricing
- separate fare rate cards by:
  - county
  - town/service zone
  - vehicle category
  - effective date
- Embu pricing must not become the permanent nationwide rate
- Owner can configure rates for Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru and other areas later without changing code

4. Marketplace
- Professionals, Merchants and Drivers must support nationwide county and town filters
- public profiles must display their service locations clearly
- Embu is the first active market, not the only supported location

5. Owner operations
- dashboard filters by county, town and service zone
- statistics by location
- driver availability and rides by area
- reports and incidents by area
- ability to activate a new Kenyan market through configuration rather than a code deployment

6. Compliance
- allow licence, insurance, document and operational requirements to vary by service type or location where legally required
- never assume every county has identical operational rules
- nationwide rollout remains disabled until the Owner confirms legal and operational readiness

7. Data model and APIs
- use stable location IDs, not free-text-only city names
- retain readable addresses and exact coordinates
- validate that locations belong to Kenya when appropriate
- no precise ride coordinates in public APIs

8. Mobile and web UX
- launch messaging may say:
  “Launching first in Embu”
- do not describe HAPA as Embu-only
- structure onboarding so additional Kenyan towns appear automatically when activated

9. Testing
Test:
- Embu active
- another county configured but inactive
- inactive area blocks ride requests and driver online mode
- activating a second county requires configuration only
- separate rate cards work in two service zones
- users cannot receive offers from the wrong zone
- nationwide search and location filters remain secure

The final product objective is:

Phase 1 — controlled Embu pilot
Phase 2 — expansion to selected Kenyan cities and counties
Phase 3 — nationwide HAPA availability across Kenya

Preserve the focused Embu pilot launch, but build the system so expansion across Kenya does not require redesigning the core architecture.
