5. M-PESA ACCOUNTING
==================================================

For an M-Pesa ride where HAPA receives the customer payment:

- HAPA receives the gross fare
- HAPA retains its commission
- Driver net earnings become HAPA owes Driver
- no cash receivable from Driver is created

Example:

- gross: KES 346.13
- commission: KES 51.92
- HAPA owes Driver: KES 294.21
- Driver owes HAPA: KES 0.00

A real payout or approved settlement reduces HAPA owes Driver.

Pending, failed or duplicate callbacks must not reduce the payable.

Do not claim a payout succeeded without a real payout/provider confirmation.
