4. CASH-RIDE COMMISSION ACCOUNTING
==================================================

Implement correct cash accounting.

When the Driver confirms cash collected:

1. Record the full fare as:
   Cash collected by Driver.

2. Record the Driver’s fare earnings.

3. Record the HAPA commission as:
   Commission due from Driver to HAPA.

4. Do not create a HAPA payout payable for the Driver’s net fare earnings.

5. Apply the commission against the Driver Commission Reserve when enabled and funded.

Example:

Gross cash:
KES 346.13

Driver earnings:
KES 294.21

HAPA commission:
KES 51.92

When Commission Reserve contains sufficient funds:

- reserve debit: KES 51.92
- Driver owes HAPA: KES 0.00
- HAPA owes Driver: KES 0.00

When the reserve has insufficient funds:

- debit the available reserve amount
- create the remaining Driver-to-HAPA receivable
- do not create an uncontrolled hidden negative balance
- show the exact outstanding amount
- preserve an immutable breakdown

Example with reserve KES 20.00:

- reserve debit: KES 20.00
- Driver owes HAPA: KES 31.92

Cash ride accounting must never display:

HAPA owes Driver KES 294.21

because the Driver already received the money directly from the customer.
