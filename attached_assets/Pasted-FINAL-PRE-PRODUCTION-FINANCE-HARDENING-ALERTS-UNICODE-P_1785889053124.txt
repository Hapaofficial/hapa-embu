5. CROSS-MONTH ACCOUNTING RULES
==================================================

Implement and prove correct month-boundary behavior.

Core rule:

- ride revenue, commission, Driver earnings and tips accrue in the month of the underlying ride/payment event
- settlements and payouts occur in the month they are completed
- finalized prior statements must never be rewritten because a later settlement occurred

Required example:

July:

- two cash rides
- Driver owes HAPA: KES 103.84
- no settlement in July

July closing:

- Driver owes HAPA: KES 103.84
- HAPA owes Driver: KES 0.00
- Reserve: KES 0.00

August opening:

- Driver owes HAPA: KES 103.84
- HAPA owes Driver: KES 0.00
- Reserve: KES 0.00

If the Driver pays HAPA KES 50.00 in August:

August movements:

- Driver-to-HAPA settlement: KES 50.00

August closing:

- Driver owes HAPA: KES 53.84

The July finalized statement must remain unchanged.

If the remaining KES 53.84 is paid later:

- the settlement appears in the later month
- Driver owes HAPA becomes KES 0.00
- no previous finalized statement is edited

Apply equivalent rules to:

- HAPA-to-Driver payouts
- partial settlements
- full settlements
- Reserve offsets
- settlement reversals
- refunds
- adjustments
- M-Pesa payables
- tips
- credit notes
- debit notes
