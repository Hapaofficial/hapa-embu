HAPA v1.6 role fix

What changed
- OWNER_EMAIL is now the single source of truth for the Owner account.
- At every app startup, every other accidental owner is automatically changed to customer.
- The account matching OWNER_EMAIL is promoted to owner and kept active.
- A database index prevents more than one owner from existing.
- Legacy partner/driver/merchant roles are safely migrated to customer roles.

Required Render environment variable
OWNER_EMAIL=Trader2027@protonmail.com

Deployment
Upload the full project contents to the GitHub repository, commit to main, then in Render choose Manual Deploy > Deploy latest commit.

Expected result
- Trader2027@protonmail.com: owner
- Moreentrader@gmail.com: customer
- Moreen keeps her current account status and password.
