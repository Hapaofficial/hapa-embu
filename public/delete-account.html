Delete your HAPA account

You can permanently delete your HAPA account at any time. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account

  1. Open HAPA (website or the mobile app) and log in.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Scroll to Delete account permanently.
  4. Confirm with your password and type DELETE.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, phone number and profile photo
  • Your public professional, shop and driver profiles, listings and photos
  • Your saved device notification tokens
  • All active login sessions

What is retained

Records that HAPA is required to keep for safety and legal reasons — such as moderation and audit history, and completed request records — are retained, but they no longer contain your personal details (your entries show as "Deleted user").

Cannot log in?

If you can no longer access your account, contact HAPA support through the contact details published on the Help page of the app or website and request deletion. Support will verify that you own the account before deleting it.
